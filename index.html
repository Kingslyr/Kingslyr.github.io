How We Work

Our Approach

Phase 01
Scoping & Baseline Study
We begin with a thorough environmental baseline — identifying key impact areas, regulatory requirements, and stakeholder mapping specific to your project site.
Phase 02
Data Collection & GIS Analysis
Field surveys, satellite imagery, GIS spatial mapping, and laboratory analysis combined to build a precise, data-driven environmental profile of your site.
Phase 03
Impact Evaluation
Using international methodologies we quantitatively assess impacts across air quality, water resources, biodiversity, soil integrity, and social dimensions.
Phase 04
Mitigation & Regulatory Reporting
Actionable mitigation plans and regulatory-ready reports satisfying NEPA, provincial EPAs, and international lender requirements — delivered on schedule.
